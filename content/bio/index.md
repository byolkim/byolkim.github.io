Byol Kim is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics at Sookmyung Women's University, which she joined in September 2024. Before that she was a postdoctoral scholar in the Department of Biostatistics at the University of Washington and a UW Data Science Postdoctoral Fellow at the eScience Institute, working with Ali Shojaie and Zaid Harchaoui.

She earned her PhD in Statistics from the University of Chicago in 2021, advised by Rina Foygel Barber and Mladen Kolar, with a thesis titled *Uncertainty Quantification under Weak Assumptions*. She also holds an MS in Statistics from the University of Chicago, advised by Lek-Heng Lim, and a BA in Applied Mathematics from Yale, awarded *cum laude*.

Her work develops inference that leans on as few assumptions as possible. Recurring threads include assumption-lean inference, predictive and conformal inference for machine-learning algorithms, tests for algorithmic stability, and differential-network analysis for high-dimensional and time-series data. Her methods have been applied in settings ranging from Markov networks to breast DCE-MRI reconstruction.
