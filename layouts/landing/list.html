Byol Kim is a statistician who thinks a lot about doing statistical inference without a whole lot of assumptions. She loves it when she can pinpoint the one structural fact a problem cannot do without—be it sparsity in a difference or invariance under permutations—and build inference on that alone. She would like to hear from researchers who work with dependent data (e.g., time series, spatial statistics, network-structured data) or with distribution shift, and who are exploring deep learning techniques but still want honest error bars. She would also love to hear from scientists in psychometrics or learning psychology who could use a statistician’s eyes.
